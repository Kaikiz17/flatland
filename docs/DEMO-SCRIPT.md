# Flatland demo — 90-second shot list

Do not record until the cards, People’s Pick template, and live worker are verified. Strip all metadata from the final file. 16:9, ~90 seconds, voice over or tight captions. Live URL on screen from the first second: https://flatland.flatland.workers.dev

| Time | Shot | What to show / say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Home, wide | Open the live site. “Flatland is an open 1-wallet-1-vote election kit. Every vote burns $WIF.” Hold the hero and the three doors: Elections, Stocks, Sign. |
| 0:08–0:20 | Election #1 room | Cut to `#/e/1`. Read the bitcoin-price question, the options, the 1 $WIF cost, the prize line. “One wallet, one vote. Vote from a wallet or just send $WIF to an option’s address.” |
| 0:20–0:35 | Cast a vote (test clip or existing confirmation) | If a wallet is funded, vote a second option on a throwaway election — not a mainnet action unless the owner has approved it. Otherwise freeze the already-voted state on election #1 and say “this vote already burned 1 $WIF on mainnet.” |
| 0:35–0:50 | I VOTED card | Fill the frame with the 1:1 card. Point to Voter #0001, the pick ribbon, the truncated wallet, the date, $WIF burned, the on-chain seal. Click **Download**, then **Share on X** (do not have to post). “A collectible pass, not a receipt.” |
| 0:50–1:00 | Sign | Open `#/rails`. Read the tagline once: petitions, RSVPs, allowlists, access gates; one $WIF act, one per wallet, on-chain streak. If no live gate, show the create-Sign control and a pre-rendered I SIGNED card (petition + RSVP palettes). |
| 1:00–1:12 | Stocks | Open `#/basket`. One sentence: the community stock pot — people put $WIF in, the vote decides what gets done. Do not send a tx. |
| 1:12–1:22 | People’s Pick | Open `#/create?template=peoples-pick`. Show the prefilled hackathon names with Flatland first, and the banner that this is a community vote on the World is Flat entries. Do not create the election in the take unless the owner has said to. |
| 1:22–1:30 | Close | Back to home, repo and live URL on a card or caption. “Built on Robinhood Chain. Unaudited beta. The flywheel is simple: to act, you use $WIF.” |

Notes: no Robinhood logo or wordmark in the title card. If a take includes a seed phrase, a key, or a secrets panel, throw it away and reshoot. Keep cuts under two seconds once the tour starts.
